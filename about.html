About My Work

I am a researcher at IIT Gandhinagar working at the intersection of building science, environmental engineering, and sustainability policy. My primary focus is on green building rating systems how they are structured, what they actually achieve, and how they can better serve India's vast diversity of climates, building typologies, and socioeconomic contexts.

India is home to some of the world's most ambitious green building targets, yet the gap between certified performance and actual performance remains striking. My research aims to understand this gap through post-occupancy evaluations, energy simulations, and comparative policy analysis.

Green building rating systems should explicitly address the trade-off between energy efficiency and indoor environmental quality (IEQ). True sustainability must balance future environmental goals with the **health and well-being of occupants in the built environment.
