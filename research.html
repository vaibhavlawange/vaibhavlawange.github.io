● Ongoing 2022 – Present
Comparative Analysis of Green Building Rating Systems Across Indian Climate Zones
A comprehensive multi-site study evaluating how GRIHA, LEED India, and IGBC rating frameworks perform across India's five climate zones. Using a combination of energy simulation, field measurements, and policy analysis to identify performance gaps, credit misalignments, and opportunities for harmonisation between the three dominant systems.
GRIHA LEED India IGBC EnergyPlus Climate Zones Policy
Collaboration: TERI · MoEFCC

✓ Completed 2021 – 2023
Post-Occupancy Thermal Comfort Evaluation in GRIHA-Rated Buildings
Field study of 18 GRIHA-rated institutional and commercial buildings in hot-dry climate zones of Gujarat and Rajasthan. Combining sensor measurements with occupant surveys using ASHRAE 55 and adaptive comfort models to assess how closely certified performance matches occupant experience.

Published: Building & Environment, 2023
